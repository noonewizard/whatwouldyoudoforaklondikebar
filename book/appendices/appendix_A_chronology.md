# APPENDIX A
## Chronology of Freemasonry and Western Esotericism

Dates marked **?** are disputed or approximate. Entries in **bold** are load-bearing for
the argument of this book.

### Late antiquity

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 100–300 CE | Greek *Corpus Hermeticum* composed in Roman Egypt |
| c. 200–300 | Latin *Asclepius* |
| c. 205–270 | Plotinus; Neoplatonic emanation |
| c. 245–325 | Iamblichus; **theurgy distinguished from magic** |
| c. 500 | Pseudo-Dionysius; Neoplatonic hierarchy in Christian dress |

### Medieval

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1119 | Knights Templar founded |
| c. 1280 | *Zohar* circulates in Castile |
| 1307–1312 | Templars arrested and suppressed |
| 18 March 1314 | Jacques de Molay burned |
| **c. 1390** | **Regius Manuscript — earliest Old Charges** |
| c. 1410 | Cooke Manuscript |
| 15th c. | Old Charges proliferate; craft legend of Euclid and Solomon |

|---|---|
