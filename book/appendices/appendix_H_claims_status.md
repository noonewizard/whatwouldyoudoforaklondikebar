# APPENDIX H
## Claims Frequently Made About Freemasonry — and Their Evidentiary Status

A reference table. Verdicts follow Appendix G. The chapter given is where the evidence is
set out.

---

### Origins

| Claim | Status | Ch. |
|---|---|---|
| Descends from the ancient Egyptian mysteries | **Unsupported** | 6, 21, 49 |
| Descends from the Knights Templar | **Unsupported**; set aside by its own adherents at Wilhelmsbad, 1782 | 26, 49 |
| Descends from a seventeenth-century Rosicrucian order | **Unsupported, and inverted** — the influence runs the other way | 12, 28, 49 |
| Descends from the builders of Solomon's Temple | **Unsupported**; the legend is medieval trade mythology | 14, 19 |
| Descends from Scottish operative lodges of 1598–99 | **Strong** | 13 |
| Derives from the English Old Charges tradition, c. 1390 onward | **Established** | 14 |
| Was reorganised in London in the 1720s by a Newtonian and Whig circle | **Strong** | 16 |
| Was founded as a Grand Lodge on 24 June 1717 | **Disputed** — single retrospective source; 1721 is better evidenced | 15 |
