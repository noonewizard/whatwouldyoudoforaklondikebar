# AUDIT PROTOCOL

Every chapter passes five audits before it is marked complete. Results are logged in
the matching file in this directory, one row per chapter.

## 1. HISTORICAL AUDIT (`historical_audit.md`)
Chronology · names · dates · organisations · titles · degree systems · geography ·
quotations · bibliographic data. Every date re-checked against a cited source, not
against the chronology file (which is itself a draft).

## 2. CITATION AUDIT (`citation_audit.md`)
Every note resolves to a real, consulted edition. Page numbers verified against the
stated edition. Primary/secondary classification correct. **No quotation of a
quotation** — the characteristic failure mode of this literature. Any surviving
`[SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED]` marker blocks completion.

## 3. ESOTERIC AUDIT (`esoteric_audit.md`)
- Kabbalah / Christian Cabala / Hermetic Qabalah kept distinct?
- Alchemy not reduced to psychology?
- Hermeticism not treated as one unchanged tradition?
- Rosicrucianism not assumed to be an organisation before it was one?
- Occult interpretations not presented as universal Masonic doctrine?

## 4. MASONIC AUDIT (`masonic_audit.md`)
- Jurisdictional differences stated where relevant?
- Historical change over time respected (1720s ≠ 1790s ≠ 1870s)?
- Craft distinguished from appendant bodies?
- Regular / irregular / co-Masonic distinctions made, and their politics named?
- No private ritual material fabricated or disclosed?

## 5. CONSPIRACY AUDIT (`conspiracy_audit.md`)
Flags: unsupported assertions · anachronism · circular sourcing · internet folklore ·
"ancient secret survives unchanged" · **argument from symbolism alone**.
The last is the most common and the most dangerous; it is checked sentence by sentence
in every symbol chapter.

## 6. PROSE AUDIT (`prose_audit.md`)
Cuts: repetitive explanation · generic AI phrasing · filler · "This chapter will…" ·
artificial profundity · rhetorical-question overuse · unsupported drama · chapter
summaries the reader does not need.

## 7. CONTINUITY AUDIT (`continuity_audit.md`)
Run across the whole manuscript at Phase 7. Checks: no fact introduced twice as though
new; terminology consistent with `research/terminology.md`; cross-references accurate;
the unresolved-questions list reconciled with Chapter 52.
