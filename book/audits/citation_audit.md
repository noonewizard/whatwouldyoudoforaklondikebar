## Anti-hallucination compliance

Checked against the brief's Rule LI. No fabricated quotations, citations, page numbers,
archival documents, rituals, organisations, dates, manuscripts or claims of scholarly
consensus have been introduced. Three specific safeguards were applied:

1. **The Pike "Lucifer" passage (Ch. 38) is paraphrased, not quoted**, with an explicit
   in-text statement of why: corrupt versions circulate that splice Pike's sentences with
   the Taxil forgery, and the wording must be transcribed from the 1871 edition or de
   Hoyos's annotated edition before it appears in print.
2. **Ramsay's Oration (Ch. 25) is paraphrased**, because the text survives in more than one
   version and the variants matter.
3. **The Ashmole diary entry (Ch. 1) is quoted** as the standard transcription, with the
   Josten page flagged for verification.

this book relies on another scholar's demonstration rather than on the underlying document

concealed in a note.
