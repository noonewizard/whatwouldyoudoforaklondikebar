evidence-level tag from the project's hierarchy:

- **[A]** Primary source (period document, manuscript, original edition)
- **[B]** Scholarly secondary source (university press, peer-reviewed)
- **[C]** Reputable modern critical edition of a primary text
- **[D]** Popular / partisan / derivative — usable only as evidence of belief

Verification tags: **✔** = bibliographic data confirmed in this research phase;
**~** = confident from standard scholarly reference but not re-verified here;
**[SVR]** = SOURCE VERIFICATION REQUIRED before citation in the manuscript.
