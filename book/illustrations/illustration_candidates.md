## PLACEMENT PRINCIPLE

Images are evidence, not decoration. Each one is captioned with (a) what it is,
(b) its date, (c) where it survives, and (d) **what it does and does not prove**. Two
images in this list — the *Rosarium* death woodcut (14) and Lévi's Baphomet (33) — are
included specifically because they are the most frequently misused images in the
popular literature, and their captions will say so.

## PERMISSIONS NOTE

Institutional reproduction fees for the Library and Museum of Freemasonry, the British
Library and the Bodleian will constitute the largest single illustration cost. A
fallback set drawn entirely from Internet Archive, Wellcome Collection (CC BY) and
Library of Congress public-domain holdings can cover approximately 20 of the 38 at
minimal cost if the budget requires it.
