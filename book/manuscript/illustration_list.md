# ILLUSTRATION LIST

Thirty-eight proposed illustrations, with holdings, rights status and chapter placement.
Full details, including the fallback set for a constrained permissions budget, are in
`illustrations/illustration_candidates.md`.

**Every item is a real, locatable object. No image has been invented.** Rights status must
be confirmed with each holding institution before publication; items marked **[SVR]**
require confirmation of shelfmark or accession number before captioning.

**Captioning principle.** Each illustration is captioned with what it is, its date, where it
survives, and **what it does and does not prove**. Two items — no. 14, the *Rosarium* death
woodcut, and no. 33, Lévi's Baphomet — are included precisely because they are the most
frequently misused images in the popular literature, and their captions will say so.
