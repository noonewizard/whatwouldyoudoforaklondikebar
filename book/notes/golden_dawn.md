# NOTE — THE GOLDEN DAWN

**Founded 1888** by William Wynn Westcott (coroner, Mason, SRIA), Samuel Liddell
MacGregor Mathers (Mason, translator, ritualist) and William Robert Woodman (Mason,
SRIA Supreme Magus). All three Freemasons. This is not incidental; it is the
explanation.

**The warrant problem.** The Cipher Manuscripts and the "Fräulein Anna Sprengel"
correspondence supplied the claim to German Rosicrucian authority. Howe (1972) showed
the Sprengel letters spurious. The Ciphers remain unattributed; the evidence points to
a nineteenth-century compilation. **Butler (2011)** reframes the question decisively by
reconstructing the founders' actual libraries — Hockley's, and Westcott's Hermetic
Library of 286 items weighted toward seventeenth- and eighteenth-century cabalistic,
Rosicrucian, Hermetic and Neoplatonic sources. What they had on the shelf accounts for
what they produced. No German order is required.
