# NOTE — KABBALAH

**The three-term discipline** (enforced by `research/terminology.md`):

1. **Jewish Kabbalah.** *Sefer Yetzirah*; the *Zohar* (Castile, c. 1280); Cordovero;
   Luria and the Safed school. Sephirot, emanation, divine names, the letters as the
   substance of creation. Scholem, Idel, Dan.
2. **Christian Cabala.** Pico's *Conclusiones* (1486); Reuchlin (1494, 1517); Knorr von
   Rosenroth's *Kabbala Denudata* (1677–84). Jewish material read to prove Christian
   doctrine. Coudert.
3. **Hermetic Qabalah.** Lévi (1850s); Mathers's *The Kabbalah Unveiled* (1887, an
   English translation *of Knorr's Latin*); the Golden Dawn; Crowley. Adds tarot
   correspondences, elemental and planetary attributions, and a grade structure —
   **none of which has any Jewish precedent.**

**The transmission chain, stated exactly:** Zohar → Pico/Reuchlin → Knorr (Latin) →
Mathers (English, 1887) → Golden Dawn. Four removes, two languages, and a systematic
reworking at the last step. It is a real lineage. It is not continuity of content.

**The Masonic question.** Kabbalistic *vocabulary* enters via the high degrees from the
1740s and is massively amplified by Lévi and by Pike (who takes it from Lévi). The
Royal Arch's concern with the divine name has a genuine biblical-Kabbalistic resonance
worth examining carefully — and its origin is an open question (Unresolved no. 4).

**Prohibited:** calling Hermetic Qabalah ancient; calling tarot-Sephirot correspondences
traditional; treating "Kabbalah in Masonry" as a single phenomenon.
