# NOTE — MASONIC SYMBOLS: DATING TABLE

For each symbol: documented Masonic meaning · earliest attestation · pre-Masonic
antecedent · later occult overlay · verdict under the Parallel-Is-Not-Descent rule.

| Symbol | Masonic meaning | Earliest attestation | Antecedent | Occult overlay | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Square | Morality, rectitude of action | Old Charges/craft usage; explicit moral reading by the 1720s catechisms | Craft tool; guild heraldry; classical *norma* | Elemental/geometric correspondences (19th c.) | Craft-internal; moral reading is Masonry's own |
| Compasses | Circumscribing desire; boundary of conduct | 1720s–30s catechisms and exposures | Craft tool; *Deus geometra* iconography | Solar/lunar, active/passive polarity (Lévi onward) | Craft-internal; the polarity reading is 19th c. |
| Level / Plumb | Equality; uprightness | 18th-c. ritual | Craft tools | Minimal | Craft-internal |
| Rough / Perfect Ashlar | The unimproved and improved self | 18th-c. ritual | Craft practice | Alchemical *prima materia* analogy (19th c.) | Resemblance, not descent |
| Two Pillars (Jachin, Boaz) | Strength and establishment | Early catechisms; named in exposures | **1 Kings 7:21 — direct biblical citation** | Kabbalistic Severity/Mercy pillars (Lévi, Mathers) | Biblical descent documented; Kabbalistic reading is 19th c. |
| Solomon's Temple | The work, the self, the ideal | Old Charges (as craft origin); ritual centre by 18th c. | 1 Kings, 2 Chron., Ezekiel; Villalpando | Astral/inner temple (19th–20th c.) | Biblical + Renaissance-architectural; **not** archaeological |
| Letter G | Geometry; God | 18th c.; **English-language specific** | — | Gematria, *Yod*, generative principle (19th c.) | **Anachronism test:** a symbol turning on an English initial cannot be ancient |
| Blazing Star | Providence; the light | 18th-c. tracing boards | Star iconography generally | Pentagram/Sirius readings (Lévi onward) | Overlay, not origin |
| All-Seeing Eye | Divine omniscience | Late 18th c.; **dating needs tracing-board research** | Christian providence iconography (Eye of Providence, widely used) | "Illuminati" reading — entirely 20th c. | Christian-iconographic; the conspiracy reading is modern |
| Jacob's Ladder | Ascent through virtues | 18th–19th c. tracing boards | **Genesis 28** | Sephirotic ladder (19th c.) | Biblical descent; Kabbalistic mapping is later |
| Acacia | Immortality; the found grave | With the Hiramic legend (by 1730) | Shittim wood of the Tabernacle | Resurrection symbolism generally | Craft-internal |
| Three Great Lights | VSL, square, compasses | 18th c. | — | — | Craft-internal |
| Sun / Moon | Lodge officers; regulation of time | 18th c. | Universal | Alchemical Sol/Luna (19th c.) | Resemblance; the alchemical mapping is an overlay |
| Numbers 3 / 5 / 7 | Degrees, senses, liberal arts | 18th c. | Pythagorean, biblical, and craft-practical alike | Numerological systems (19th c.) | **Over-determined** — a number's recurrence proves nothing |
| Darkness / Light | Ignorance to knowledge | Earliest ritual | Universal religious grammar | Illumination doctrines (19th c.) | Universal; useless as a descent argument |
| Death / Raising | Moral transformation | 1730 (Prichard) | Dying-god patterns; alchemical putrefaction; Christian resurrection | Egyptian Osiris identification (19th c.) | **The key test case of the whole book** |

**Standing rule for Chapters 20–22:** a symbol's presence in an older tradition is
evidence about the older tradition. To be evidence about Masonry it needs a route.
