# CHRONOLOGY
*Freemasonry, Magic, and the Western Esoteric Tradition*

Dates marked **?** are disputed or approximate. Entries in **bold** are load-bearing
for the book's argument. The column "Status" records evidentiary confidence.

**Interpretive note.** The Old Charges give Masonry a *legendary* genealogy running

claim, and it long predates any esoteric reading of it.

## THE THREE LAYERS TO KEEP SEPARATE

1. **When something happened.**
2. **When somebody first claimed it happened.**
3. **When that claim became widely believed.**

For most of the connections this book examines, layers 2 and 3 fall in the eighteenth
and nineteenth centuries, and layer 1 does not exist at all.
