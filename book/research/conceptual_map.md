# CONCEPTUAL RELATIONSHIP MAP

Twelve currents; every link classified. The classification is the argument.

## Classification key

| Tag | Meaning | Evidentiary test passed |
|---|---|---|
| **[D] DOCUMENTED** | Named people, datable transmission, surviving texts | All twelve source-criticism questions |
| **[P] PROBABLE** | Chronology permits; personnel overlap; no direct document | Chronology + opportunity, no smoking gun |
| **[C] CONTESTED** | Competent scholars disagree | Evidence genuinely cuts both ways |
| **[S] SUPERFICIAL PARALLEL** | Resemblance only | Fails the transmission test |
| **[U] UNSUPPORTED** | Asserted; no evidence; often chronologically impossible | Fails outright |

1. Hermeticism (ancient + Renaissance)
2. Kabbalah (Jewish) / Christian Kabbalah / Hermetic Qabalah
3. Alchemy
4. Rosicrucianism
5. Christian mysticism and theosophy (Böhme, Pietism, Martinism)
6. Freemasonry (Craft)
7. Masonic high degrees (Écossais, Templar, Rose-Croix, Royal Arch)
8. Illuminism (Élus Coëns, Strict Observance, RER, Bavarian Illuminati)
9. Theosophy (Blavatsky)
10. Golden Dawn
11. Ceremonial magic (Lévi → Mathers → Crowley)
12. Modern esotericism (Thelema, Wicca, contemporary occulture)

## THE LINKS

### Into Freemasonry (the hard direction)

| From → To | Tag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Renaissance art of memory → Scottish lodge (Schaw circle) | **[C]** | Stevenson's claim; suggestive, thinly documented |
| Old Charges craft legend → Craft ritual | **[D]** | Textual continuity is direct and demonstrable |
| Hermeticism → Craft ritual, pre-1717 | **[S]** | Shared vocabulary of geometry and light; no transmission document |
| Alchemy → Craft ritual | **[S]** | Ashmole was both alchemist and Mason — a *person*, not a channel |
| Rosicrucianism → Craft ritual, pre-1717 | **[U]** | Contemporaneous, not connected. The link runs the other way and later |
| Christian Kabbalah → Craft symbolism | **[S]** | Temple/pillar/name imagery is common biblical stock |
| Anderson's deism/Newtonianism → 1723 *Constitutions* | **[D]** | Berman, Jacob: the strongest documented ideological input |
| Biblical text (1 Kings, 2 Chronicles) → Temple symbolism | **[D]** | Direct and explicit |

**Summary of this block: the esoteric currents did not build the lodge.** What built
the lodge was a craft legend, a Scottish statutory system, and an Enlightenment London
ideology. This is the book's most important negative finding.

|---|---|---|

| Alchemy/Rosicrucian texts → Gold- und Rosenkreuz degrees | **[D]** | McIntosh; the order *required* Masonic membership |

### Out of Freemasonry (the easy direction)

| From → To | Tag | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Masonic degree structure → SRIA (1866) | **[D]** | Masonic membership was an entry requirement |
| SRIA → Golden Dawn founders | **[D]** | Westcott, Mathers, Woodman all SRIA |
| Masonic grade/temple/officer architecture → Golden Dawn ritual | **[D]** | Form borrowed; content is Kabbalistic-Hermetic and new |
| "Sprengel"/Cipher MSS → German Rosicrucian descent | **[U]** | Warrant spurious (Howe); Ciphers a 19th-c. compilation |
| Lévi → Pike's *Morals and Dogma* | **[D]** | de Hoyos's annotations make this unanswerable |
| Lévi → Golden Dawn, Blavatsky, Crowley | **[D]** | Direct textual dependence |
| 1840s French socialism/Catholic heterodoxy → Lévi's magic | **[D]** | Strube 2016 — the revision that resets the whole revival |
| Golden Dawn → A∴A∴ | **[D]** | Crowley's own restructuring of GD material |

**Summary of this block: from 1860 onward, influence runs overwhelmingly *out* of
Masonry into occultism, not *into* Masonry from antiquity.**

| Claim | Tag | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|

| Templars → Freemasonry (1314 → 1717) | **[U]** | 400-year void; the claim itself dates from the 1730s–50s |

## THE SHAPE OF THE WHOLE

Read as a diagram, the map is not a tree with an ancient root. It is an **hourglass**:

- **Upper chamber (antiquity → 1700):** many separate currents — Hermetic, Kabbalistic,
  alchemical, Rosicrucian, mystical — with no single trunk and no route into the lodge.
- **The narrow waist (c. 1717–1782):** Freemasonry, having been built out of other
  materials entirely, becomes the era's dominant **form** for initiatory association.
  The esoteric currents are then poured *into* that form, degree by manufactured degree.
- **Lower chamber (1800 → present):** everything downstream — Rosicrucian revivals,
  the Golden Dawn, Theosophical Co-Masonry, the O.T.O., Thelema, Wicca — inherits the
  Masonic form, and reads the upper chamber's contents backwards through it.

The book's thesis lives in that waist. Freemasonry's relationship to magic is not one
of descent. It is one of **carriage**: Masonry did not inherit the Western magical
tradition, it gave that tradition a body to walk around in.
