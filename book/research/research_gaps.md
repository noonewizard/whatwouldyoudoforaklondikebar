# RESEARCH GAPS — WORK REQUIRED BEFORE AND DURING DRAFTING

Ordered by urgency. Each item names the gap, why it matters, and how to close it.

## TIER 1 — BLOCKS SPECIFIC CHAPTERS

1. **Prescott & Sommers, exact citation.** *Ars Quatuor Coronatorum* volume and pages
   for "1717 and All That," plus the *REHMLAC+* version of "Searching for the Apple
   Tree." The Quatuor Coronati domain is egress-blocked in this environment.
   *Close by:* REHMLAC+ open access (Universidad de Costa Rica) or an academic library.
   **Blocks Ch. 15.**
2. **Élus Coëns and the Régime Écossais Rectifié.** Martinez de Pasqually's *Traité de
   la réintégration des êtres* (edition history), Willermoz's papers (Bibliothèque
   municipale de Lyon), and the French scholarship (Amadou, Vivenza, Var).
   **Blocks Ch. 27** — the chapter carrying the book's single strongest claim about
   operative magic entering a Masonic structure.
3. **Rite of Strict Observance documents.** Von Hund's original charters and the
   Wilhelmsbad (1782) proceedings, in German. **Blocks Ch. 26.**
4. **African Lodge No. 459 archival material.** Prince Hall's petitions and
   correspondence; the 1784 warrant; holdings in Boston (Grand Lodge of Massachusetts;
   Massachusetts Historical Society). **Blocks Ch. 32** from being written from
   secondary sources alone.
5. **Golden Dawn Cipher Manuscripts.** Current custodianship, published facsimiles, and
   the state of the paper/ink analysis. **Blocks Ch. 41** from making a firm statement.

## TIER 2 — IMPROVES CHAPTERS SUBSTANTIALLY

6. **Iamblichus, *De mysteriis*** — critical edition (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell, SBL
   2003 **[SVR]**). Ch. 7 is currently thin.
7. **Villalpando**, *In Ezechielem explanationes* (1596–1604) — the Renaissance Temple
   reconstructions that stand between the Bible and the lodge. Ch. 19.
8. **A critical text of Ramsay's Oration** — the several manuscript and printed versions
   differ, and the differences matter. Ch. 25.
9. **Early tracing boards and lodge inventories** — dating the first appearance of
   specific symbols (Blazing Star, All-Seeing Eye, Jacob's Ladder) in material culture
   rather than in commentary. Chs. 20–21 depend on this for their datings.
10. **French and German Masonic scholarship in the original** — Mollier, Porset,
    Beaurepaire, Kervella; the *Renaissance Traditionnelle* corpus.
11. **Morgan affair primary press** — Batavia and western New York newspapers, 1826–28.
    Ch. 33.
12. **Priory of Sion documentation** — the 1956 registration and the BnF *dossiers
    secrets*. Ch. 50.

## TIER 3 — DESIRABLE

13. Jewish responses to the Masonic use of Kabbalah (beyond Katz, 1970).
14. Twentieth-century American Masonic membership statistics from Grand Lodge returns.
15. The Co-Masonic archives (Le Droit Humain) on the Besant period.
16. Comparative initiation material outside the European frame — for the explicit
    purpose of *not* claiming descent (Ch. 44).
17. Manly P. Hall's sources, as a case study in how popular esotericism is assembled.

## STANDING METHODOLOGICAL GAPS

- **Every page number** in the bibliography marked **~** must be verified against the
  stated edition before it appears in a note.
- **Every quotation** must be checked against the original edition, not a quotation of
  a quotation. Circular sourcing is the characteristic failure of this literature.
- **Every date** in the chronology must be re-checked at the fact-check phase.
