## AUDIT 2 — ATTRIBUTION

**Checked:** who actually said what.

**Result: PASS with corrections already applied.**

- The Isis–Osiris–Horus reading of the 3-4-5 triangle is **Plutarch's** (*De Iside* §56),
  not a Masonic tradition. Its application to Masonry is nineteenth century. Corrected in
  `controversies.md` §B1 before it could enter a chapter.
- The "cathedral effect" is **Meyers-Levy and Zhu (2007)**, not a general finding of
  architectural history.
- The severity-of-initiation finding is **Aronson and Mills (1959)** — one small study,
  not a body of literature.
- Two placeholder bibliography entries carrying invented-looking author names were
  identified and replaced with real works (Meyer 1987; Clauss 2000).

## AUDIT 6 — PSYCHOLOGY

**Checked:** modern theory separated from historical belief.

**Result: PASS, and this was the audit that changed the most.**

- `research/psychology_matrix.md` gives every finding its population, its inferential
  distance, and its replication status.

## AUDIT 7 — SYMBOLISM

**Checked:** documented meaning separated from later interpretation.

**Result: PASS.** `research/symbolic_system_map.md` processes 25 symbols through the
eight-step protocol. Every occult reading is dated; every conspiracy reading is dated.

**Pattern confirmed from Book I:** documented Masonic meanings are biblical, craft-derived or
morally allegorical; esoteric readings are overwhelmingly nineteenth century; conspiratorial
readings are twentieth. The square's eight layers are set out as the worked example.

**One symbol changes status in Book II:** the 47th Proposition, which Book I did not treat.
It is **documented in Masonry from 1723** — earlier and more explicitly than most Masonic
symbols — while its Egyptian gloss is a later graft. It is the most interesting single case
in the symbolic map.
