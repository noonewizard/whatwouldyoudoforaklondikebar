# CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE — BOOK II

Book I's chronology traced the *transmission* of esoteric material. Book II's traces the
development of the **ritual apparatus** and, in a second column, the emergence of the
**disciplines that can analyse it** — because part of this book's subject is the fact that
the tools for answering its question are only about a century old.

**?** = disputed. **Bold** = load-bearing.

## III. THE OBSERVATION THIS TIMELINE MAKES

Set the columns beside each other and something becomes visible.

**The technology was complete by 1730. The vocabulary for analysing it did not exist until
1909, and the experimental programme not until the 2000s.**

Which means every historical interpretation of Masonic ritual — Preston's in 1772,
Oliver's in the 1820s, Pike's in 1871, Waite's in 1911, Leadbeater's in 1926 — was made
without any of the analytic tools this book will use, by people describing an effect they
could feel and had no framework for.

That is not a reason to dismiss them. It is a reason to read them as **primary evidence of
the effect** rather than as failed theories of it. When Preston writes at length about what
the ceremony does to a candidate, he is not doing bad psychology. He is a participant
reporting, in the only language available, on the working of a machine nobody could yet
describe.

**Book II's method follows from this: read Layer 1 as phenomenology, and Layer 6 as theory,
and never confuse the two.**
