# PSYCHOLOGY / RITUAL RESEARCH MATRIX

Per brief §XI (Type D) and §LII (Audit 6). **Every row states the finding, the population
it was established on, the inferential distance to eighteenth-century Freemasonry, and the
replication status.**

The last column is the one that matters. A book that quotes psychology at history without
it is doing the thing this project exists to criticise.

---

## I. RITUAL AND MEMORY

| Finding | Source | Population | Distance to our material | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emotionally arousing events are encoded and retained more durably than neutral ones | Standard memory literature | Laboratory; broad | **Short.** A general property of human memory; the ceremony is arousing by design | **Robust** |
| Infrequent, high-arousal ritual produces vivid episodic memory and interpretive diversity; frequent low-arousal ritual produces doctrinal uniformity | Whitehouse, *Modes of Religiosity* (2004) | Melanesian and comparative | **Moderate.** Framework built elsewhere; **predicts four features Masonry independently shows** | **Influential; contested in detail** |

## V. WHAT THIS MATRIX CANNOT SUPPORT

Stated as prohibitions, because the temptation is severe and constant.

1. **No claim about any named historical person.** Not Ashmole, not Washington, not Pike,
   not any candidate. We have no data and the category of claim is unavailable.
2. **No claim that an effect occurred in the eighteenth century.** Only that ritual of this
   design would, on current understanding, be expected to produce it.
3. **No claim of moral improvement.** No control group has ever existed. The ritual's own
   stated purpose is the one thing the evidence cannot address.
4. **No use of a contested finding as though settled.** Aronson and Mills and the awe
   literature are flagged here specifically so that no chapter can lean on them quietly.
5. **No inference from modern initiates to historical ones.** A modern candidate has read
   about the ceremony, lives in a different culture, and undergoes a modified working.

## VI. THE DISCONFIRMATION PROGRAMME (brief §LIII)

For the thesis *ritual produces durable change*, the following would count against it and
must be actively sought:

- Evidence that eighteenth-century Masons commonly lapsed, forgot, or treated the ceremony
  as trivial. **Lodge minutes record non-attendance and exclusion for non-payment in large
  numbers — this is real disconfirming evidence and must be reported.**
- Evidence that men who had read the exposures underwent initiation unaffected.
- Evidence of initiates who described the ceremony as disappointing. Satirical and hostile
  accounts of the period are the obvious place to look.
- The observation that the vast majority of members appear to have joined for sociability,
  charity and connection, and left no trace of interest in the symbolism at all.

**That last point is the strongest disconfirming evidence available and the book must not
bury it.** If Masonic initiation is a transformative technology, it is one whose most
common outcome was a man who enjoyed the dinners.
