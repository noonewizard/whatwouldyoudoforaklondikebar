# RESEARCH GAPS — BOOK II

Ordered by urgency. Book I's outstanding items carry forward where still relevant.

## TIER 1 — BLOCKS SPECIFIC CHAPTERS

1. **Anderson 1723 on the 47th Proposition.** The exact wording, the page, and whether the
   figure appears on the frontispiece or title page. The claim circulates in Masonic
   literature in several forms. **Blocks Ch. 18**, which is a major chapter.
   *Close by:* a facsimile or digitised copy of the 1723 edition.

2. **Replication status of Aronson and Mills (1959).** The severity-of-initiation finding
   is the single most-cited experimental result in discussions of fraternal initiation.
   Before any chapter relies on it, its replication record must be established from the
   literature, not from secondary summary. **Blocks Chs. 10 and 44.**

3. **Replication status and effect sizes of Meyers-Levy and Zhu (2007).** The "cathedral
   effect" is in the same position. **Blocks Ch. 46.**

4. **Royal Arch primary material.** The degree turns on the recovery of a lost divine name
   and is central to Ch. 24. Its origin is unresolved and its published sources are thinner
   than the Craft's. **Blocks Ch. 24.**

5. **Preston's lecture system in a reliable edition.** Preston is Book II's richest
   Layer-1 source — a contemporary attempt to say what the ritual *does*. The editions
   differ substantially between 1772 and 1812 and the differences matter. **Affects 14
   chapters.**

6. **Lodge minutes, charity accounts and inventories as primary evidence.** Chapters 44–46
   propose to test claims about behaviour against records. Those records must be identified
   and consulted, not invoked. Candidate repositories: Library and Museum of Freemasonry,
   London; Grand Lodge of Massachusetts; the Scottish Rite Masonic Museum, Lexington.

7. **Plutarch, *De Iside et Osiride* §56** — exact text and a reliable translation, for the
   Isis–Osiris–Horus triangle in Ch. 18.

## TIER 2 — IMPROVES CHAPTERS SUBSTANTIALLY

8. **A history of geometry as a discipline** for Ch. 17, currently flagged thin.
9. **Webb's *Freemason's Monitor* (1797)** and the American lecture tradition — the
   divergence from Preston matters for the jurisdictional-variation rule.
10. **The Lodge of Reconciliation's revisions (1813–16).** The one occasion on which English
    ritual was deliberately and collectively redesigned, by a named committee, with
    surviving records. **Directly relevant to Ch. 6's question about whether the ritual was
    designed.** This is the best available evidence on the "deliberate technology"
    question and it has not yet been examined.
11. **Tracing-board dating.** Carried over from Book I: the earliest datable appearance of
    specific symbols in material culture rather than in commentary.
12. **Pigpen cipher in actual Masonic use.** Certificates, lodge records, correspondence —
    to establish what it was used *for*, which is Ch. 38's factual base.
13. **Nineteenth-century Masonic temple architecture.** Commissioning records, architects'
    statements, and building-committee minutes, to test the four candidate explanations in
    `architecture_matrix.md` §IV.
14. **French and German ritual scholarship in the original** — Dachez, *Renaissance
    Traditionnelle*, and the German lecture traditions.

## TIER 3 — DESIRABLE

15. Whitehouse's *Inheritance* (2024) — publication details and whether it modifies the
    modes-of-religiosity framework.
16. Contemporary satirical and hostile accounts of Masonic initiation, as disconfirming
    testimony on the ceremony's impact.
17. Prince Hall and Adoption ritual texts, to test whether the mechanisms in the ritual map
    hold across jurisdictions with different content.
18. Masonic funeral and corner-stone ceremonies — public ritual, which the ritual map does
    not currently cover.

## STANDING METHODOLOGICAL GAPS

- **Every page reference** marked **~** must be verified before appearing in a note.
- **Every quotation** checked against the original edition, never a quotation of a quotation.
- **Every modern framework** applied to historical material must carry its inferential
  distance in the sentence that applies it.
- **The disconfirmation programme** in `psychology_matrix.md` §VI is a research obligation,
  not a rhetorical gesture. The evidence that most Masons joined for sociability and left no
  trace of interest in the symbolism is the strongest counter-evidence to this book's thesis
  and must be actively gathered rather than conceded in passing.

## OPEN QUESTIONS CARRIED FROM BOOK I

Still open and still relevant here: why Ashmole joined; who composed the third degree; where
the Royal Arch came from; what Schaw's art-of-memory clause meant; the earliest Masonic
All-Seeing Eye; whether "Western esotericism" is a real historical object.

## NEW OPEN QUESTIONS RAISED BY BOOK II

19. **Did men who had read the exposures experience initiation differently?** If ritual
    efficacy depends on controlled information, this is the natural test — and I know of no
    evidence either way.
20. **Why is there no historical testimony of trance or ecstasy in Masonic initiation?**
    The components are present; the reports are not. The absence looks like design.
21. **What did the Lodge of Reconciliation think it was doing in 1813–16?** The only
    documented deliberate redesign of the ritual by a named body.
22. **Does the ritual map hold for the Adoption Rite and for Prince Hall workings?** If the
    seven mechanisms are general, they should. If they are not, the thesis narrows.
