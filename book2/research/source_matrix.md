# SOURCE-TO-CHAPTER MATRIX — BOOK II

Numbers refer to `citations/bibliography.md`. **Bold** = load-bearing.

**Standard:** every chapter draws on ≥1 Level-A primary source and ≥2 Level-B scholarly
sources. Chapters that cannot meet it are flagged **[THIN]**.

**Additional standard for Book II:** any chapter applying a modern psychological or
architectural framework must also cite the source of the *caveat* — the replication
problem, the population difference, or the contrary reading. Those are marked **⚠**.

## FLAGGED CHAPTERS

- **Ch. 17 [THIN]** — needs a history of geometry as a discipline. Candidate: a standard
  history of mathematics; to be identified.
- **Ch. 24 [THIN]** — Royal Arch primary material. The degree's origin is an open question
  (Book I, ch. 52) and its ritual sources are less accessible than the Craft's.
- **Chs. 10, 44, 46** — cannot be drafted without the caveat literature in hand. The
  replication status of Aronson and Mills (136) and of Meyers-Levy and Zhu (138) must be
  established, not assumed.
- **Chs. 44, 45, 46** — require lodge minutes, charity accounts and inventories as primary
  evidence. Currently unsourced at the document level.
