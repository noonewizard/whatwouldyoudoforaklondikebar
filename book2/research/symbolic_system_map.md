## SYMBOLIC COMPRESSION — THE WORKED EXAMPLE

The brief (§XIX) asks for a theory of how one object carries many meanings. The square is
the test case, and the layers can be dated.

| Layer | Meaning | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A tool for testing a right angle | Immemorial | Trade fact |
| 2 | The emblem of a trade | Medieval | Heraldic |
| 3 | The practical form of a liberal science | Old Charges, c. 1390 | Craft legend |
| 4 | Rectitude of conduct | By 1720s | Documented Masonic |
| 5 | The standard that does not care who you are | 18th-c. moral lectures | Documented Masonic |
| 6 | A badge of membership | 18th–19th c. | Social |
| 7 | Matter, the passive pole, the square of the elements | 1850s onward | Occult overlay |
| 8 | A conspiratorial marker | 20th c. | Modern projection |

**Eight layers, of which four are documented Masonic, two are pre-Masonic trade facts, and
two are later additions.** The compression is real. It is also *historically stratified*,
and the strata can be dated — which is the demonstration Book II owes the reader.

**The theoretical claim to be tested in Chapter 39:** symbolic compression is not a mystical
property. It is what happens when a concrete object is used by many people for a long time
in a context that refuses to fix its meaning. The refusal is the mechanism.
