## THE COMPOSITE — RULES OF USE

Book I established a labelled composite reconstruction of an eighteenth-century initiation
from published exposures. **Book II uses the same composite and the same labelling, and does
not extend it.** No new ritual detail is introduced that is not in a source cited in
`citations/bibliography.md` §I.

**Standing statement, to appear in the front matter and again in Chapter 2:** this is a
reconstruction from sources published between 1723 and 1762; practice varies by
jurisdiction, period and rite; no working described is current or universal; no private
ritual material is reproduced or invented.

## THE ADDITION NO STAGE-MODEL CAPTURES

The candidate does not undergo the ceremony once. He watches it performed on others for
years, from progressively senior positions, and eventually performs it himself.

**This may be the most consequential feature of the whole system and no model in the ritual-
studies literature accounts for it.** Chapter 47 is where it is argued.
