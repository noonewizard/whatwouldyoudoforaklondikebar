# APPENDIX I
## The Hidden-Message Research Protocol

The ten tests of Chapter 42, in a form that can be applied.

This is the book's practical contribution and it is not specific to Freemasonry. It
applies to any claim that something is concealed in a text, a name, a number, a
building or a symbol.

**It is not a machine for saying no.** Two of the four worked examples pass.

---

## THE TESTS

| # | Test | The question | Fatal if failed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Provenance** | Where did the material come from, and who vouches for the chain? | Serious |
| 2 | **Dating** | When was the alleged encoding performed? Date the artefact, not the tradition. | **Fatal** |
| 3 | **Encoding rule** | Is the cipher documented independently of the claim? | Serious |
| 4 | **Precommitment** | Was the method — *and the acceptance criterion* — specified before the result was seen? | **Fatal** |
| 5 | **Controls** | Does the identical procedure, on matched material that cannot contain the message, yield comparably? | **Fatal** |
| 6 | **Base rate** | How often does a meaningful output arise by chance, given the search space and the acceptance criterion? | Serious |
| 7 | **Replication** | Can a stranger, given only the stated method, reproduce the result unaided? | Serious |
| 8 | **Plausibility** | Could the alleged author have used this method? | **Fatal** |
| 9 | **Alternatives** | Is there a simpler account? | Weakening |
| 10 | **Intentionality** | Is there evidence the pattern was *put there*, independent of the pattern itself? | **Decisive** |

### Scoring

Failing **4** or **5**: no evidential weight, however striking the output.
Failing **2** or **8**: dead.
Failing only **9**: may be true; not yet demonstrated.
Passing all ten: demonstrated.

### Two notes on the hard tests

**Test 5 requires *matched* controls.** Matched in search-space size, not merely in
subject. A control given fewer chances than the claim is not a control. This book's
own first attempt failed exactly here, and the failure is printed in Chapter 16.

**Test 4 extends to the answer key.** Not only the procedure but the criterion for
what would count as a meaningful output must be fixed in advance, by someone who
does not know the answer. This is the subtlest of the ten and the one most often
missed by careful people.

## WORKED EXAMPLES

### 1 — The Bible Code — **FAILS**

Drosnin (1997) claimed equidistant letter sequences in Genesis encode modern
events.

Fails 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. Passes only 1.

**Test 5 ended it.** McKay, Bar-Natan, Bar-Hillel and Kalai applied the same method
to other texts and found assassination "predictions" for Indira Gandhi, Yitzhak
Rabin, both Kennedys, Martin Luther King, Abraham Lincoln and Leon Trotsky — in the
English text of *Moby-Dick*.

*Statistical Science* 14, no. 2 (1999): 150–73. **The model for this entire
protocol.**

### 2 — Atbash / Baphomet → Sophia — **FAILS**

**Passes 3** (Atbash is genuinely attested) **and 7** (the transformation
reproduces exactly: בפומת → שופיא).

Fails the rest.

**The computation, in three stages, reported in the order it was performed:**

*Stage one.* BAPHOMET enumerated against control names; controls returned zero.
**The control was invalid**: BAPHOMET's spelling pattern carried three optional
*mater* positions, giving it **216** admissible spellings against 4–72 for the
controls. A larger search space finds more. This was a failed control presented as
a passed one.

*Stage two.* Skeletons matched. **Controls: 0 hits in 777 spellings. Baphomet: 1 in
216.** Base rate for a random five-letter Hebrew string hitting a target: analytic
**0.000116%**, confirmed by 200,000 Monte Carlo trials (seeded, reproducible). On
this showing the claim survives.

*Stage three.* The target list was examined. **It had been written by the author,
knowing the answer**, and *Sophia* — a Greek word in defective Hebrew
transliteration, absent from any Hebrew lexicon — was on it only because the claim
put it there. **Removed: Baphomet returns 0 hits, identical to every control.**

**Finding: the transformation is real; the interpretation carries no weight,
because the success criterion was set after the result was known.**

All three scripts are published with this book, including the one containing the
error.

### 3 — Pigpen gravestone inscriptions — **PASSES**

Ten for ten. Physical monuments, located and dated. Rule documented independently
and fixed before reading. Survives controls. Produces continuous text. Replicates in
a stranger's hands. Historically plausible. Somebody carved it.

**What it encodes is a name and a date.** A genuine cipher, genuinely used,
demonstrably intentional, carrying nothing secret.

### 4 — Enochian — **PASSES, category corrected**

Passes provenance, dating, rule, replication and intentionality: manuscripts in
Dee's hand, sessions dated 1582–89, tables reproducible, the whole thing written
down.

**But it is a produced language, not a recovered one.** The evidence was good enough
to establish not merely that a claim was false but what kind of claim it actually
was — which is the best outcome a test can deliver.

## THE PENTAGRAM CONTROL

Reported in Chapter 43. Method fixed before execution.

A 12 × 8 lattice of **96 points** with uniform jitter of ±0.03 units — the spacing
of an ordinary street grid, containing nothing by construction. **400,000** random
placements of a regular pentagram (centre, circumradius, rotation) tested for
vertices falling within 0.5 units of a lattice point; best result refined by local
optimisation.

**Result: maximum vertex deviation 0.20 grid units — 5.7 per cent of the
circumradius.**

At any normal drawing scale the line weight swallows that error. Reproduced as a
figure with five points marked and the other ninety-one omitted — which is how such
figures are always reproduced — it would look like a finding.

**The method finds pentagrams in anything.** That is not an argument that no
building has designed geometry. It is the observation that retrospective fitting
returns a positive result either way, and **a test that cannot come back negative is
not a test.**

## THE DISCIPLINE, IN FOUR LINES

**State the procedure before you run it**, in enough detail that a stranger could
execute it without asking a question.

**Count the attempts, including the ones you abandoned.** Keep the list. It will be
embarrassing, which is what it is for.

**Build the control first** — before you know what the real material will yield, and
before the exciting result has made the control an obstacle.

**And notice who wrote the answer key.** If the criterion for success was set by
someone who already had the result, nothing has been tested, however rigorous
everything downstream looks.

---

## THE STANDING DISTINCTION

**A symbol can acquire meaning its maker never intended.** A fact about reception,
not about origin.

**A reader can discover a real interpretation that was never placed.** Legitimate,
and not a discovery about the author.

**A coincidence can be meaningful and remain a coincidence.** Meaning is not scarce.

**An intentionally encoded message requires evidence of intentional encoding**, and
no quantity of output can supply it.
