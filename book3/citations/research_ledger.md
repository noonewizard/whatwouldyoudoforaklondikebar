## LEDGER DISCIPLINE

Three rules, enforced at draft:

1. **No E5 or E6 claim appears as factual prose.** Where such a claim is discussed,
   the text names whose claim it is and what it fails.
2. **Every E3 is marked as interpretation in the body text**, not merely in a note.
3. **E4 items are reported as disputes**, with both sides attributed — never resolved
   by authorial preference.
