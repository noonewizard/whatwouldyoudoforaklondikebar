## THE THREE ORIGINAL DIAGRAMS

**55 — The Atbash table.** The 22 letters paired, with the two biblical
attestations worked beneath: ששך → בבל and לב קמי → כשדימ. Caption states that
these are the only undisputed occurrences and that both are Hebrew-to-Hebrew proper
nouns.

**56 — The control-test figure.** Two columns. Left: BAPHOMET's 216 admissible
Hebrew spellings, one highlighted. Right: the same procedure on matched control
names, nothing highlighted. **Beneath, in the same weight, the third panel: the
target list, with *Sophia* circled and the annotation "added by the claim."**
Caption: *the transformation is real; the answer key was written afterwards.*

**57 — The pigpen grid.** The layout, a worked inscription, and the letter-frequency
histogram of plaintext and ciphertext side by side — identical, which is the flaw.

## CAPTIONING PRINCIPLE

Every caption states what the object is, its date, where it survives, and **what it
does and does not prove**. Items 49 and 50 (Rosslyn; the Washington plan) are the
two most misused images in this literature and their captions name the claim and
state the objection. A picture makes an argument whether or not the text endorses
it, and the caption is the only place to answer it.

retained under any scenario, because Chapter 26 turns on them.
