## A NOTE ON METHOD

This book makes claims of several different kinds, and they are not
interchangeable. A statement about what a document says is one sort of thing. A
statement about what a ritual does to a person is another. A statement about
whether a word has power in the world is a third, and this book does not make
statements of that third kind in either direction.

Where a claim rests on evidence, the evidence is named. Where it rests on
interpretation, the text says so. Where the sources disagree, the disagreement is
reported rather than resolved by preference. Six levels of evidential strength are
used throughout and set out in Appendix H.

Every cipher result, every gematria figure and every probability in this book was
computed rather than copied, and the scripts that produced them are published with
it. Two of those scripts contained errors during the research. Both are described
in the text where they occur, because a book that asks its readers to check
other people's work has no business hiding its own corrections.

## A NOTE ON RITUAL

No private ritual material is reproduced here, and none is invented. The Masonic
material quoted comes from sources that have been publicly in print since 1730 —
the printed exposures, the published constitutions, and the ritual editions that
any bookseller could supply.

Masonic practice differs between jurisdictions, between constitutions, and between
periods. Where this book describes a ceremony it identifies which tradition it is
describing. Nothing here should be read as an account of what all Masons do, because
there is no such account to give.

## A NOTE ON THE HEBREW

Hebrew words are given in the Hebrew alphabet with transliteration. The
transformations performed on them in Parts III and IX were carried out by machine
and checked against standard published figures.

The author's Hebrew is not independent. Where a reading depends on philological
judgment, the text names the scholar whose judgment it follows. This is stated
once here and not repeated, but it applies throughout.
