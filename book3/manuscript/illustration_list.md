## THE THREE ORIGINAL DIAGRAMS

**55 — The Atbash table.** The eleven pairs, with both biblical attestations worked
beneath: ששך → בבל and לב קמי → כשדימ. Caption states that these are the only
undisputed occurrences, that both are Hebrew-to-Hebrew, and that both are proper
nouns whose plain forms appear elsewhere in the same book.

**56 — The control-test figure.** Three panels. Left: BAPHOMET's 216 admissible
Hebrew spellings, one highlighted. Centre: the same procedure on matched control
names, nothing highlighted. **Right, in the same weight: the target list, with
*Sophia* circled and annotated "added by the claim."**
Caption: *the transformation is real; the answer key was written afterwards.*

**57 — The pigpen grid.** The layout, a worked inscription, and the letter-frequency
histograms of plaintext and ciphertext side by side — identical, which is the flaw.

## CAPTIONING PRINCIPLE

Every caption states what the object is, its date, where it survives, and **what it
does and does not prove.**

Items 49 and 50 (Rosslyn; the Washington plan) are the two most misused images in
this literature, and their captions name the claim and state the objection. A
picture makes an argument whether or not the text endorses it, and the caption is
the only place to answer it.

Item 20 (the Masoretic pointing on the Tetragrammaton) carries the book's most
important caption: **these are the vowels of a different word, and reading them here
produces "Jehovah."**
