# INDEX FRAMEWORK

A structure for the professional indexer. Page numbers cannot be assigned before
typesetting; what follows is what the index must contain and which distinctions it
must preserve.

**Three principles govern it.**

1. **Distinctions the book insists on must survive indexing.** Kabbalah, Cabala and
   Qabalah get three entries, not one with variants. An index that merges them
   undoes Part VII.
2. **Claims the book rejects are indexed under their own names**, with the location
   of the assessment. A reader looking up "Baphomet" should find it and find the
   answer.
3. **Methodological terms are indexed as fully as historical ones**, because the
   method is half the book.

## NOTE ON LENGTH

A book of this density supports an index of roughly 12–16 printed pages.
If space is constrained, reduce section A to principal discussions only.
**Sections E, F and H are not reducible** — they are what makes the book usable as a
reference, and section H in particular is load-bearing.
