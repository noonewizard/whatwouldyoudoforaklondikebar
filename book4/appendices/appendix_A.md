# APPENDIX A
## The Five-Level Model

This book distinguishes five things that are all called "the Temple." Confusing
any two of them produces claims that sound historical and are not. The model is
used throughout and is set out here in one place.

| Level | What it is | Evidence available | Where treated |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The building** | A physical structure on a ridge in Jerusalem, Iron Age | No identified remains; comparative Levantine architecture; disputed tenth-century stratigraphy | Part I, chs 1–5 |
| **2. The text** | The descriptions in 1 Kings, 2 Chronicles, Ezekiel, Middot, Josephus | The texts themselves, composed centuries after the events, disagreeing on numbers | Parts I–II; App. B, F |
| **3. The symbol** | The Temple as cosmos, as body, as diagram, as origin of architecture | Datable: each reading has authors and periods | Parts II, IX, XI, XII, XIV |
| **4. The ritual setting** | The declared location of a ceremony — the lodge as Temple | Documentary: exposures, catechisms, constitutions from the 1720s | Parts VII, VIII |
| **5. The inner Temple** | A person, understood as a structure under construction | Textual from 1 Corinthians onward; psychological claims rated separately | Part IX, ch 87 |

### The rules that follow from it

**A claim about one level is not a claim about another.** "The Temple is a model of
the cosmos" is well supported at level 3 for the first century CE (Josephus says
so) and unsupported at level 1 as a statement about builders' intentions.

**Movement between levels runs in one direction and is datable.** Building → text →
symbol → ritual setting → inner Temple. Each transition has a period, and none
runs backward. Chapter 34 dates the level-3-to-level-4 transition in Freemasonry
to the 1720s–30s.

**Level 1 is the only level that can be lost.** The others survive its destruction,
and Chapter 90 argues that they survive *because* of its destruction.

**Most false claims in this subject are level confusions.** "Freemasons want to
rebuild the Temple" reads a level-4 or level-5 statement as a level-1 project.
"The Temple encodes the golden ratio" reads a level-3 conviction as a level-1
measurement. Chapters 63 and 73 respectively.
