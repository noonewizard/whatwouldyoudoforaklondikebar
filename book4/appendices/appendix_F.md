# APPENDIX F
## The Temple's Stated Dimensions

All figures in cubits, as the Masoretic text gives them. The cubit's length is not
fixed with certainty; Newton devoted a dissertation to establishing it (ch 68).

### The house (1 Kings 6)

| Element | Length | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| The house | 60 | 20 | 30 |
| The porch | — | 20 across the front | 10 deep |
| The inner sanctuary | 20 | 20 | 20 |

Ratios of the house: **3:1** (L:W), **2:1** (L:H), **2:3** (W:H). All small
integers (ch 71).

The inner sanctuary is a stated cube. Note that at 20 cubits it does not reach the
30-cubit height of the house on the plain reading; how the vertical gap was used is
disputed (chs 3, 7).

Side-chamber tiers: 5, 6, and 7 cubits (1 Kings 6:6, 6:10).

### The pillars — the four-source discrepancy

| Source | Pillar height | Chapiter | Circumference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Kings 7:15–16 | 18 | 5 | 12 |
| 2 Kings 25:17 | 18 | **3** | — |
| 2 Chronicles 3:15 | **35** | 5 | — |
| Jeremiah 52:21–22 | 18 | 5 | 12 |

Proposals: Chronicles reports the pair's combined length; a scribal error;
different sources measuring differently. No consensus (ch 38).

**This table is the single most important piece of evidence against any
hidden-code claim.** A transmission that could not preserve the difference between
18 and 35 did not carry a cipher (ch 71).

### The molten sea (1 Kings 7:23, 7:26)

Diameter 10, height 5, circumference given as 30. True circumference for diameter
10 is 31.4159; implied π = 3.0; error **−4.51%**.

The rim is separately described as a handbreadth thick with a flared brim. If the
30-cubit line measured outside the rim and the 10-cubit diameter the bore, both
figures hold and no approximation is involved. This resolution is discussed in the
rabbinic tradition (Eruvin 14a **[SVR]**) and is not the only proposal; this book
does not adjudicate (ch 71).

### The whole numerical corpus

Eleven values: 60, 20, 30, 20, 10, 20, 20, 20, 5, 6, 7. Seven distinct. Every
hidden-code claim about Solomon's Temple is a claim about these numbers.
