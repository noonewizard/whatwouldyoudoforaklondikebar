### The finding

Of the major Masonic buildings in the world, effectively **none** is an attempt to
reproduce Solomon's Temple.

This is not for lack of means, information, or ambition. These are expensive,
architect-designed buildings commissioned by institutions with money, at a time
when Villalpando's engravings had been in print for three centuries and the
biblical dimensions were in every Bible.

The conclusion drawn in Chapter 65: the Temple was never an architectural
programme. It is a ritual and mnemonic frame, and a frame requires enclosure, a
controllable door, and room to move a candidate about in. Everything else is the
architecture of the period.
