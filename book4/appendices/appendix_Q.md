# APPENDIX Q
## Psychology Cited, with Replication Status

Part X applies an explicit rule: **a finding is reported as established only if it
is a constraint, a large and repeatedly observed effect, or has survived direct
replication.** Everything else carries its status. This appendix is the register.

### The rule behind the register

The claim "the building shapes your thinking" is, in its experimental form, a
social priming claim — and social priming is the area of psychology most damaged by
the replication crisis of the 2010s.

Two findings in this table would have strengthened this book's argument
considerably had they been reported in their strong form. Both are reported weak.
