### How to read this register

**A flag is not a concession that the claim is wrong.** It records that the book's
own verification standard was not met at that point, and says what would meet it.

**Nothing load-bearing depends on a flagged item.** The findings in Appendix E do
not rest on any entry above. Entries 6 and 7 support a caution rather than a
claim — they are flags on findings the book *declined* to lean on. Entry 11 is a
flag on somebody else's assertion.

**The register exists because the alternative is worse.** A book with no visible
gaps is either exhaustively verified or concealing something, and the second is far
more common than the first.
