## SCORING NOTE

Per the master prompt's explicit instruction, **no overall numerical "truth score"
is assigned** to any claim; the Evidence Level column classifies the STRONGEST
supportable version of each claim, and where a claim bundles a true component with
a false one (several rows above), both levels are given rather than collapsed into
one misleading figure.
