## THE FOUR ORIGINAL DIAGRAMS (17, 18, 58/59, 60, 62 — five total)

Made for this book, exactly as Books II and III each included original control
diagrams. Each is captioned to state plainly that it is a diagram constructed for
this book, not a historical artifact, and each exists to let the reader see a
control test's result rather than merely read about it — the same rationale
Book III gave for its Atbash control figure.

## CAPTIONING PRINCIPLE (carried from Books II–III)

Every caption states what the object is, its date, where it survives, and **what
it does and does not prove.** Items 5 (Megiddo "Solomonic" gates), 43–44
(Villalpando), and 67 (any physical museum Temple model) are the images most
likely to be mistaken for historical fact by a casual reader, and their captions
state the dating dispute or the reconstructive/artistic status explicitly.
