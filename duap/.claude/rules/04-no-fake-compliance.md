# No fake compliance claims

The protocol records assertions and produces evidence. It does not make
anyone compliant with anything, and no artefact in this repository may
suggest otherwise.

## Requirements

1. Every regulatory statement cites the specific instrument and provision
   (for example "Regulation (EU) 2016/679 art. 30(1)(b)"), not the
   instrument alone.
2. Every mapping row is marked as one of:
   - **technical** -- the protocol produces an artefact that is mechanically
     relevant to the requirement;
   - **interpretation-required** -- whether the artefact satisfies the
     requirement is a legal judgement.
3. `COMPLIANCE.md` opens with a statement that it is not legal advice, and
   that statement is not qualified elsewhere.
4. A lawful-basis code in an event records what the controller asserts. No
   document may describe it as a determination that the basis applies.
5. No claim of certification, endorsement, approval or adequacy by any
   authority or standards body, unless a citation to that body's own
   published decision is given.

## Language that is forbidden without a citation

"GDPR compliant", "HIPAA compliant", "meets the requirements of", "approved
by", "certified", "adequate", "satisfies article N".
