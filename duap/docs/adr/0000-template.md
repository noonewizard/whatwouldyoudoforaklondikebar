# ADR-0000: Template

**Status:** template · not a decision

Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md`. Every section is required; an
empty "Rejected alternatives" section means the decision was not thought
through, because something was rejected or there was nothing to decide.

## Context

What is true that makes this decision necessary. Facts, not intentions.

## Problem

The question, stated so that a reader could answer it differently.

## Alternatives

At least two that were genuinely considered, described as their advocates
would describe them.

## Decision

What was decided, in the imperative.

## Trade-offs

What the decision costs. A decision with no cost was not a decision.

## Consequences

What must now change elsewhere: code, documents, processes, other ADRs.

## Rejected alternatives

Each alternative, with the reason it was rejected, stated fairly enough
that someone who preferred it would recognise their position.
