# Research register

**Status:** PRODUCTION (process artefact) · 2026-09-21

Every major unresolved question gets an entry with the same seven
headings: Problem, Known work, Hypothesis, Experiment, Result, Conclusion,
Remaining uncertainty. An entry with no experiment is allowed; an entry
with a conclusion and no result is not.

| # | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 0001 | Do data-attribution methods agree enough to price with? | **answered, negatively** |
| 0002 | Can payout avoid reintroducing linkage? | open |
| 0003 | What happens when an organisation refuses to participate? | analysed, no technical answer |
| 0004 | Can differential-privacy budgets be accounted across releases? | open |
| 0005 | Does pricing data usage increase collection? | open, unstudied |
| 0006 | How much does the multiplier policy change who pays? | **answered, positively** |

Two entries are answered and they point opposite ways, which is the most
useful thing in this register. 0001 found that attribution estimators
disagree badly enough that none can ground a payment, so attribution is
refused. 0006 asked the same question of pricing coefficients and found
the ranking survives — unanimously at the top of the distribution — even
though the amounts move by 11× at the median. The protocol therefore
prices metered usage ordinally and refuses to attribute, and both halves
of that position are now measured rather than assumed.
