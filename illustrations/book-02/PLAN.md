diagrams, blocking, and staged transformation. Where Book I draws chains across
centuries, Book II draws them across minutes.

## Keystone figures

**`@fig-sensory-lanes`** (A). Four fixed lanes across the duration of a ceremony,
showing which channels are occupied when. Built from published exposures only,
and the caption says so. It makes the book's argument — that the effect is in the
*coincidence* of channels, not in any one — visible in a way prose cannot.

**`@fig-adaptation-asymmetry`** (A). Dark and light adaptation plotted against
time. Dark adaptation takes minutes; light adaptation takes seconds. The ceremony
runs in the direction the physiology makes dramatic. Measured curve, cited source,
no interpretation in the figure itself.

**`@fig-gematria-control`** (A). Target vocabulary and control vocabulary in one
frame, same scale, with the search space in the footer. The control panel
component (Deliverable 8) exists for this figure and its siblings in Books III
and IV.

## Caution

No figure in this book may depict a ritual. Blocking diagrams show **positions
and movement described in published exposures**, abstracted to markers, with the
source named. Nothing is reconstructed beyond what those printed sources state.
