## Visual thesis

input, rule, output, and *control*. Every transformation figure in this book
shows its control in the same frame.

## THE CIPHER VISUALIZATION TEMPLATE (Deliverable 12 requirement)

Every cipher figure uses one fixed nine-field template. No exceptions, no
abbreviated variants.

```
┌── INPUT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  source text · language · script · normalization applied   │
├── ALPHABET ───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  the full alphabet used, with final forms shown explicitly │
├── RULE ───────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  stated formally; reproducible by a reader without the fig │
├── DIRECTION ───────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  forward / reverse                                         │
├── OUTPUT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  the raw result, before interpretation                     │
├── INTERPRETATION ─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  what the output is claimed to mean — visually SEPARATED   │
├── CONTROLS ────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  the same rule on matched material with no claim attached  │
├── ALTERNATIVES ────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  other rules that produce other intelligible outputs       │
└── SEARCH SPACE ───────────────────────────────────────────┘
   n attempted · tolerance · TARGET FIXED IN ADVANCE: yes/no
```

**The OUTPUT and INTERPRETATION panels are separated by the threshold rule** —
the series' recurring boundary motif. A reader must see where the mechanical
result stops and the claim begins.

**A cipher figure without the CONTROLS and SEARCH SPACE panels cannot be
registered.** The Figma component does not permit it.

## The three teaching figures

These exist to teach the reader how pattern recognition misleads. They are
priority A and among the most important figures in the series.

**`@fig-signal-vs-coincidence`** (A). One rule, one intelligible output, shown
beside the same rule applied to twenty control words — nineteen of which also
produce something. The reader sees the base rate.

**`@fig-multiple-testing`** (A). A grid of transformation space: alphabets ×
rules × directions × normalizations. Cells that produce *something* intelligible
are marked. The marked cells are numerous. Caption: *this is what a large enough
search space does, and it is why a hit must be reported with its denominator.*

**`@fig-prespecified-vs-posthoc`** (A). Two panels, identical procedure. Left:
the target was named before the search. Right: the target was chosen after
seeing the output. Same picture, opposite evidential value. This is the single
hardest idea in the series to convey in prose.
