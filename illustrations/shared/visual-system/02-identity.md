# DELIVERABLE 2 — SERIES VISUAL IDENTITY

## Register

**Target:** the drawing office and the archive reading room. Measured, tactile,
restrained. The reference points are architectural working drawings, archaeological
site plans, philological apparatus, and scientific plates — not book covers about
secret societies.

## Explicitly excluded

| Excluded | Why |
|---|---|
| Fake parchment, burn edges, sepia wash | Implies archival status the image does not have |
| Glowing symbols, lens flare, radiance | Rhetorical intensity substituting for evidence |
| Conspiracy aesthetics: red string, pinboards, "all-seeing eye" collage | The books *analyze* this genre; adopting it would endorse it |
| Fantasy engraving pastiche | Pseudo-historical; violates Principle 4 |
| Photorealistic "reconstructions" | Violates Principle 2 |
| Dark backgrounds with occult glow | Sensationalist; also fails print |

## Recurring structural motifs

Four motifs carry the identity across all six books.

**1. The measured field.** A faint baseline grid, visible at 6% tint, behind
architectural and geometric figures. It signals: *this was measured, and you can
check it.* Never decorative — always to scale.

**2. The evidence gutter.** A narrow band along the figure's bottom edge carrying
the evidence badge, source line, and figure ID. Constant position across all six
books. It is how a reader learns, by the third figure, where to look for the
warrant.

**3. The threshold rule.** A single heavier vertical or horizontal rule marking a
boundary — a wall, a veil, a door, a grade, a date, an institutional edge. The
series' recurring subject is *access*, and this is its mark.

**4. The unfinished edge.** Where a figure's content is genuinely incomplete —
what the sources do not tell us — the drawing stops, and the stroke degrades to
dotted rather than being carried to a tidy border. **Figures are allowed to end
mid-air.** This is the visual equivalent of the manuscripts' `[SVR]` flags.

## Per-book accent

One accent hue per book, drawn from the series palette, used sparingly — for the
book's own recurring motif only, never for evidence encoding (which is fixed
series-wide).

| Book | Accent | Motif it marks |
|---|---|---|
| I | Verdigris | Transmission arrows |
| II | Oxblood | Ritual sequence markers |
| III | Indigo | Cipher transformation arrows |
| IV | Ochre | Measured dimensions |
| V | Graphite-blue | Tool and process marks *(provisional)* |
| VI | Slate-green | Authority relations *(provisional)* |
